# Brag Plan: Amrita Placement Portal — Bengaluru

## What is this app?
A role-based campus placement portal where students explore and apply to openings, recruiters manage applicants, and the placement team oversees outcomes.

## The angle
A quiet, premium campus-to-career product film: begin with the supplied Bengaluru campus sketch and wordmark, then let the student mobile journey carry the story. Keep claims concrete and grounded in the screens. The app supports three roles, but the centerpiece is one verified student flow rather than a stack of feature labels.

## Hook (first 2–3 seconds)
On deep wine, the supplied Amrita wordmark resolves over the architectural campus sketch. “From campus learning to a career in motion.” uses the landing page headline verbatim.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The fresh student dashboard appears as a close crop of its “Browse opportunities” entry point; keep all displayed counts outside the frame.
- Show the fresh mobile application detail capture in its actual submitted state, using only the QA-approved synthetic demo record.

## Outro / punchline
The device recedes into the campus sketch. End on the supplied Amrita Bengaluru wordmark and “The next right opportunity.” No invented metric or unsupported availability claim.

## User flow worth showing
Student reaches the portal → opens Browse opportunities → sees an actual submitted application state. Authentication codes, personal fields, and summary counts stay out of frame. The screenshots are frozen and QA-approved.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium campus-to-career product film
- Interpretation: deliberate camera moves, restrained transitions and typography, one readable thought at a time; no fake urgency or startup parody.

## Format: landscape — 1920×1080
## Duration: 22 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project source; confirm against frozen UI)
- Background: `#431626` wine with `#f8f6f2` ivory panels
- Accent: `#761731` burgundy and `#c79a63` gold
- Text: `#30272b` ink on ivory; ivory on wine
- Video overlays: EB Garamond display and Inter body (HyperFrames bundled fonts, selected to keep the composition self-contained); the embedded product capture retains the app's own typography
- Strongest visual element: supplied Amrita Bengaluru wordmark and architectural campus sketch, paired with the final mobile interface capture

## Share copy (draft)
From campus learning to a career in motion. Amrita Placement Portal is a shared place to find the next right opportunity.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, understated music bed with sparse motion-matched UI accents
- Music: bundled `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
- Music treatment: trim to the 22-second film; low under the visuals, short fade-in and fade-out. Keep the final wordmark clean and legible.
- Music cue guidance: bundled cue preset read; estimated 109.96 BPM. Strong-cue candidates at 20.19s and 22.92s. Use the cue near the closing reveal only if the final timing allows it; don't stretch the film or rush copy.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none
- SFX posture: sparse; two soft reveal accents from bundled assets only; no simulated clicks, casino sounds, or dramatic hits
- Audio-coupled moments: restrained accents for the opening and closing brand reveals only; no UI click or notification sound
- Restraint rule: no voiceover, no code/email notification sounds, and no cues that imply a real message was sent

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Campus / next chapter — 4s
Wine field opens into the supplied campus sketch. The supplied wordmark resolves, followed by “From campus learning to a career in motion.” No fabricated location footage or extra claims.
Sequential/interaction: no
Audio intent: warm arrival, restrained
Audio-coupled idea: one soft logo reveal accent

### Scene 2 — Browse opportunities — 6s
A macro crop from `dashboard-mobile.png` reveals the actual top app bar, greeting, and “Browse opportunities” button. The crop ends before the dashboard cards, so it contains no displayed counts. Show this as a zoomed capture detail rather than a fabricated full phone screen.
Sequential/interaction: one screen reveal, no simulated taps unless the final capture sequence provides evidence
Audio intent: calm confidence
Audio-coupled idea: no separate SFX; let the screenshot settle into the music
Music: low and steady
Transition mood: soft slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Application submitted — 7s
Use a large crop from the QA-approved `apply-mobile.png` capture. Begin on the actual opportunity details, then pan down the same frozen screenshot to its real “Application submitted” confirmation and “follow its progress in Applications” message. It contains synthetic demonstration data and no personal names or emails. The camera pan is the only motion; do not simulate a tap or a state change.
Sequential/interaction: one capture, panned from role details to its existing submitted-state message; no simulated tap or state transition
Audio intent: a subtle lift through the workflow
Audio-coupled idea: no click or notification SFX; let the submitted state read clearly over the music
Music: warm and restrained
Transition mood: crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Campus-to-career — 5s
Return to the campus sketch and supplied wordmark. “The next right opportunity.” closes the film, using copy from the landing page. No invented URL, result statistic, or launch status.
Sequential/interaction: no
Audio intent: resolve gently
Audio-coupled idea: final reveal may settle near the 20.19s cue if exact edit timing supports it
Music: short resolved tail and fade
Transition mood: soft fade to ivory → end

**Music mood for this video:** warm, polished, lightly rhythmic
